Overview

Why Bharathiar University?

For working professionals and graduates who cannot commit to a full-time campus programme, the Distance MBA from Bharathiar University represents one of the more credible options in India. It combines the reputation of a nationally ranked public university with the practical flexibility of Open and Distance Learning (ODL).

Established in 1982 in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, Bharathiar University is a State Government institution recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). Its distance education wing — the Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), previously called the School of Distance Education (SDE) — has been delivering management education since 1991–92.

"The Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) was founded in the academic year 1991–92 to provide students with the opportunity to pursue higher education without attending regular on-campus classes." — Official CDOE, Bharathiar University

The MBA offered through CDOE is UGC-DEB approved, meaning the degree carries the same national recognition as a regular postgraduate qualification from any Indian university.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

The eligibility criteria for the Distance MBA are straightforward and inclusive. As per the official CDOE admission notice:

"Candidates who have completed study under 11+1+3 / 10+2+3 / 10+3(Diploma)+3 alone are eligible for PG / PG Diploma programmes." — Official CDOE Admission Notice, Bharathiar University

In plain language, this means:

  • A Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised Indian university is sufficient.
  • No entrance examination is required. Admission is purely merit-based on graduation marks.
  • No age restriction applies to the distance MBA programme.
  • Lateral entry is available for candidates who already hold an MBA and wish to pursue a different specialisation.

How to Apply

Step-by-Step Admission Process

The admission process is fully online. Bharathiar University has not authorised any private agency, franchise, or third-party study centre to facilitate admissions — all applications must be submitted through the official portal.

Generate Your ABC & DEB IDs

Before applying, create your Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) ID and Distance Education Bureau (DEB) ID. These are UGC-mandated for all ODL admissions in India and must be submitted with the application.

Visit the Official CDOE Portal

Go to sde.b-u.ac.in and navigate to the Online Learning Programme (OLP) section. Do not apply through any third-party website or agent.

Fill & Submit the Online Registration Form

Complete the application form with personal, academic, and contact details. Pay the non-refundable application fee of INR 150 through the digital payment gateway.

Upload Required Documents

Scan and upload all required documents (listed below). Ensure all scans are legible and within the portal's specified file size limits.

Pay Course Fee & Receive Confirmation

After the application is submitted and fee is paid online, the university issues an admission confirmation. Keep this confirmation for all future reference.

Learning Infrastructure

Support for Distance Students

Distance learning does not mean studying in isolation. Bharathiar University's CDOE provides a structured support system:

Learning Support Centres (LSCs)

The CDOE operates 300+ approved Learning Support Centres across India. These centres provide students with access to academic guidance, library facilities, and peer interaction without requiring travel to the main campus in Coimbatore.

Personal Contact Programmes (PCPs)

The university conducts Personal Contact Programmes at specified centres in Coimbatore and at approved LSCs nationwide. PCP attendance is optional but recommended for students who want structured interaction with faculty and classmates.

Study Materials & LMS

Students receive study materials in both print and digital format. Access to the university's Learning Management System (LMS) supports self-paced learning throughout the programme.
